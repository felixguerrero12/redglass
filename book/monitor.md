#### Ongoing Monitoring

In a rapidly changing, probabilistic world, analytical conclusions are always tentative. Te situation may change, or it may remain unchanged while you receive new information that alters your understanding of it. Specify things to look for that, if observed, would suggest a signifcant change in the probabilities. Pay particular attention to any feeling of surprise when new infor- mation does not ft your prior understanding. Consider whether this surprising information is consistent with an alternative hypothesis. A surprise or two, however small, may be the frst clue that your under- standing of what is happening requires some adjustment, is at best in- complete, or may be quite wrong.
